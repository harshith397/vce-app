# VCE Companion 🎓

A clean, friendly mobile companion created by students, for students of Vasavi College of Engineering.

### Why We Built This

Checking attendance and marks on the college portal can feel like a chore—getting logged out every few minutes, pinching and zooming tiny tables on your phone, and trying to calculate percentages on paper before exams.

We built **VCE Companion** to make your daily college life simpler, faster, and stress-free.

---

### What It Does For You

* **Smart Bunk Predictor:** Never guess your attendance. The app instantly tells you how many classes you can safely skip while staying above 75%, or exactly how many classes you need to attend to get back on track.
* **Period-by-Period Day View:** See your entire daily schedule (Periods 1 to 7) in clean, easy-to-read cards. Instantly spot which class was marked present or absent without squinting at web tables.
* **Subject-Wise Breakdown:** View your attendance percentage for every subject individually so you know exactly which classes you cannot afford to miss.
* **Internal Marks & SGPA:** Check your CIE tests, lab marks, and semester grades without waiting for slow portal pages to load. Your SGPA is calculated automatically.
* **Stays Logged In:** Open the app and see your records in two seconds between classes—no repeated logins, session timeouts, or captchas.
